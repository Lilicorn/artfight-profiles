 Kréature is the craziest little uhh… creature? you’ve ever met!! Even if you don’t need him, he will always be there for you!
Crawling out of a swamp one day, Kréature found himself surrounded by animals that weren’t too fond of his existence :( He used to be aggressive, and constantly argued with others just to defend himself. All he wanted was for someone to finally love him for who he is - and that “someone” turned out to be Pillou.

Kréature used to be a lizard - a quite clumsy one at that. He was usually alone, either sunbathing or running around (as "daily cardio"). He often lost his tail unintentionally, and Kréature just couldn't figure out why that happens.

As he grew older, he didn't feel comfortable being a lizard, and wanted something different. Specifically, he wanted to fly. He often admired the flying bugs instead of eating them, and preferred to chew the plants instead - which wan't the usual meal for his type.

One day, he decided to follow a fly on its route, because he wanted to see the way flies live. As he chased it down, they got to a swamp, which was very far away from Kréature's usual home. While he was scared, he was also mesmerised by the coolness and the mystery of this place.

The fly flew through to get to the other side of the swamp.

And so did Kréature.

Or... atleast he tried to.

Kréature fell into the swamp, pretty much suffocating in the water despite being able to swim. He did this all to himself. And he let it happen. He felt the cool waterdrops absorbing his skin as he sunk deeper and deeper. It felt so bittersweet. So calming. As the water reached his eyes, he finally fell asleep. And the flies couldn't do anything but watch it.

...

The memory of a lizard ever being stupid enough to not swim out of a lake quickly faded away as time passed by. Therefore it was a huge surprise for the resident flies to see a dragon-like creature crawling out of the (now even steeper) water one day. It was Kréature's true self.

The purple-ish animal looked around, only to find himself in the same spot again. He looked at his reflection in the water, and was delighted to find that he has completely changed. He has wings now!! And a much cooler tail! Turning yellow from happiness, he started flying around at rapid speed in excitement.

As he further discovered himself and tried to make friends this way, he was yet again dissapointed in the world. The others kept telling him to stop being so loud and to leave them alone. Kréature, not knowing social cues, kept going against their will which has resulted in many heated arguments / fights. He became aggressive, and has spent most of his days defending himself. Anytime he met someone new, he had negative expectations (...somewhat rightfully), which was only oil on the fire.

That was until he met this weird girl; that was approaching him unintentionally.
Pillou was minding her buisness when Kréature started chewing on her right leg. She shaked him down hesitantly, as Kréature hissed back as usual. Pillou told him to calm down, and that she is not as hurtful as others may think. And it worked like a charm. The animal completely changed; his skin went from a salmon red to a light yellow, and he almost stopped moving completely.

Pillou tamed him. She was the first person to do so. Pillou started calling him Kréature, since none of them really know what exactly he is.

Kréature now spends all his time with Pillou if he can. Reason being is that Pillou was the only one ever that has appreciated him the way he is, so he is thankful for that.
