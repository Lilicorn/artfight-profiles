Randomized Super - This Brawler starts with a random Super that keeps changing after every use.

 Attack: Lily's Top 3

Lily 2 switches between three attacks: first a powerful swing from Bibi, then a blinding light from Buster, and a sugary-sweet treat from Mandy to end things off.

Similar to Mina. Except Lily 2 goes from short to long range.

Damage: 2800 -> 2760 -> 2600

Range: Short -> Normal -> Long

Reload speed: Normal (in all occasions)

 Super: Be Who You Wanna Be

Lily 2 mimicks the super of a random brawler on the map, by using her colorful imagination.

  • Damage depends on the mimicked Super
  • Super charges in 7 hits (in all occasions)
  • Super recharge depends on the mimicked Super’s damage

Note: Lily 2 cannot copy the super of another Lily 2. If all brawlers happen to be a Lily 2, the super will be chosen randomly from all the brawlers currently avaible in the game.
