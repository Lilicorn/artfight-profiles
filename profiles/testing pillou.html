 Pillou was originally a toy doll handcrafted by a manufacturer in France. Not many copies were sold of her, nor did those copies end up as butterfly mutants - making Pillou truly one of a kind!
She now lives alongside the tiny animals that live near Blank's (her former owner's) garden. She has trouble getting along with them, due to her appearance and orange wings being "scary" according to "normie" insects. Pillou's best friend (and only friend for now) ended up being another misfit, who she just calls "Kréature". Together, they now frolic in the fields✨

Pillou was originally a toy doll handmade in a small factory. Pillou was one of the few dolls of the product line. Her design got the least sales due to her “tomboyish” design, in fact, she only got sold once - as Blank’s birthday gift.

Blank loved the uniqueness of Pillou and Blank took care of her dearly. Pillou didn’t really have feelings at this time, but I’m sure their friendship was something special. They’ve spent many days, months, even years together.

That was until an unfortunate day, Pillou got lost in Blank’s garden while her family was coming back from a trip.
While for most people this wouldn’t be an issue, Blank’s garden was huge. Her family was insanely rich and their “garden” technically meant a field, a lake and a nearby forest. Blank searched for her beloved doll day and night everywhere and yet she still couldn’t find her. As more days passed, Blank eventually got a new doll as a replacement, and Pillou was forgotten.

…

Pillou slowly became one with nature as time quickly passed by. Her wooden body has now merged into the branches of a smaller tree, and only her head remained visible.

Then, a guest appeared.

It was a caterpillar searching for a place to pupate. And it thought that this exquisite branch was perfect.

And just like every (not) normal butterfly, this caterpillar became one with its branch, also known as Pillou.

Now being (perhaps) the largest monarch butterfly, Pillou crawled out of her pupa a few weeks later. Not knowing what to do and what she’s capable of. All alone.

…

She got used to it eventually. But the residents of nature didn’t. She was so drastically different from an insect that everyone was (and still is) scared of her. And that’s without taking the wings into consideration. Those large, orange, toxic wings. All those disappointments made her believe that the flora and fauna only existed to hate her.

…

It was one of these days when she noticed an unfamiliar face chewing on her right leg. She shaked it down hesitantly, as the entity hissed back. Pillou told him to calm down, and that she is not as hurtful as others may think. And it worked like a charm. The animal completely changed; his skin went from a salmon red to a light yellow, and he almost stopped moving completely.

Pillou tamed him. She was the first person to do so. And what do you do with a pet that you just got? Name them. And that’s how Kréature is now called Kréature.

Pillou now spends all her time with her new comapnion, willingly or unwillingly. They are always there for eachother, even when no one else is.
