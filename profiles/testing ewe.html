 Ewe is yet another butterfly mutant, but also a failure at that - she’s somehow stuck as a catepillar forever. Her insect simps don’t seem to be mad about that though… She’s an arrogant, sassy fashion icon, who happens to be Pillou's biggest fear for no particular reason. Like, they’ve only met once or twice and Pillou still hates her. Jealousy much? Or is it still the ongoing rivalry between their manufacturers? Perhaps Kréature is the problem? I guess we will never know. #badwriting

Ewe was originally a toy doll handcrafted in a quite famous doll factory. Ewe's design was also called Eve, but with a 'V' this time. She was already popular before she became her current form; so it's evident that a lot of kids had atleast one Eve in their "collection". [human design coming next year, i promise]

One day, some clumsy kid lost their own copy of Eve in a forest that happened to be close to Blank's (Pillou's former owner's) garden.
Time passed by, and Eve pretty much became one with the flora and fauna. So much so that she has merged into a bush's branches. That "bush" later grew up to be a beautiful tree.

And that's when a sudden newcomer appeared - a caterpillar! It was planning to pupate just on the sam exact tree that Eve was "laying on".

Caterpillar + Eve = Ewe. Kaboom. Except Ewe never became a butterfly.

She was fine with it though. And so was everyone else, surprisingly. Ewe used her doll-like beauty to lure a bunch of fine-lookin' insects around her and bully the ugly ones. Kréature was also one of the "uglies" according to her, and she's cleary caused a lot of bad days for him.
