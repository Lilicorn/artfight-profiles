  • ⚠️ All fouras are monochrome, ranging from dark blue to white.
    • This is the most important rule!
    • The outline color [#211c66] is the darkest blue of them all.
    • The darkest color in a foura's design is this blue: [#2a2b95]
    • Very little amount of fouras have other colors in them, or have the ability to "emit" any sort of color.
    • Most fouras have white skin color [#ffffff]. Darkskin fouras have a blue(r) skintone; with the darkest possible being this blue [#6d79ce].

  • ⚠️ All fouras have a paper-like aura to them!!
    • The paper outline's thickness is about 1/4 of the face's width (front view).
    • The outline doesn't act like a hard border / hitbox. Anytime a foura collides with an object, the outline dissapears accordingly.
    • The outline is mostly smooth; but turns spiky if a foura turns angry/agressive.
    • The paper texture is mostly visible on white parts of a foura. The darker the spot, the less you see any paper.

▶ their job cycle (yes, they have jobs)
  • Fouras all have these job cycles (yet to figure out a proper name for them) which they're recommended, but not obligated to take.
    • most fouras do their "jobs" but some boycott it
  • Fouras can take a job once every 28 foura days / 1 human week.
  • Fouras can use their devices to spy on a human from above before they start their job. This way they have a clear plan as to where to go and who to catch.
  • Once they are allowed to; they leave Fourapolis and travel to the human world in a human disguise. 
    • This disguise is usually their previous stage 1 self; but some fouras switch bodies for fun. Either way they basically reincarnate as a dead person
  • They then have four human days to 1) rizz someone up and 2) turn them into a foura with a consensual kiss.

If they completely airball it:

  • The "victim" stays in the human world, and nothing happens to them. At most they will be confused as to where their previous "Tinder match" went.
  • The foura goes back to Fourapolis, and most likely gets laughed at.
  • The foura has to kill off their human disguise once the time runs out. In other words, they have to kill themselves.
    • Some fouras like this part of the job, but most find it hard and traumatizing.
  • The foura will also have one added to their death counter, thansk to the reasons explained above.
  • The foura can try again in 1 human week / 28 foura days.

If they succeed / turn someone into a foura:

  • The foura gets one added to their death counter.
  • The "victim" immeadietly dies, and enters stage 2 of life (fourism).
  • The victim gets one added to their death counter, since they literally died.
  • The victim then has yet another 4 human days / 16 foura days to decide whether they want to stay in this world, or if they want to get revived through a potion.

The former

The latter is not a recommended solution for several reasons:

  • the revived guy

▶ novamors

Novamors are a noble version of fouras. 

Anyone can become a novamor as long as they find love as a foura beforehand.

Looks:

  • Everything stays the exact same as it was in one's foura state.
  • There is 1 singular difference though: the color scheme is different!!
    • It switches from white with dark blue outline to black [#000000] with a pink [#E002BE] outline.
      • The outline has a veery slight glow to it.
      • The skintone management also dissapears.

Extra info:

  • Their city is called Novamora.
    • It takes place in an enormous, pink cloud right above Fourapolis.
  • Other than that, it looks like a regular city in the human world.
  • As i mentioned, novamors live a hedonistic, noble lifestyle. Mansions, fancy parties, pretentious happiness, and of course, a bunch of lovebirds.
  • Acting out love is not an option. Fourism and novamorism takes the deepest, subconcious feelings into accounting.
    • So you can't pretend to be in love with someone,
    • and you may lose your novamor state before losing feelings in your conscious mind.
